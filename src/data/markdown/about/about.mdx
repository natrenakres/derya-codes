It contains the R package to estimate AIPW, IPW, and IPWRA estimators using Covariate Balancing Methods: IPT, Graham et al. You can download it via the devtools package and entering install_github("deryauysal/teffects2"). The package is based on the great work on treatment effect estimation in R by  [Oliver Hines](https://github.com/ohines/teffectsR).'

## Bio

I am an *Associate Professor* in the Department of Economics at **Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München** (LMU Munich).
My research focuses on econometrics, microeconometrics, and causal inference, with particular emphasis on treatment effect estimation and policy evaluation.
I received my Ph.D. in Economics summa cum laude from the University of Konstanz and have published extensively in leading journals including the Journal of Applied Econometrics, Journal of International Economics, and Statistics in Medicine.
I am a Research Network Affiliate at CESifo and a member of the Collaborative Research Center Transregio 190 "Rationality and Competition," funded by the German Research Foundation (DFG).
I have held visiting positions at Harvard University and New York University, and regularly present my research at premier international conferences in econometrics and applied economics.
